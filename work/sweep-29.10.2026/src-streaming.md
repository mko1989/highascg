## src/streaming/caspar-ffmpeg-setup.js
- `RISK` src/streaming/caspar-ffmpeg-setup.js:150 — `REMOVE ${ch} NDI "${ndiName}"` interpolates the source name into an AMCP quoted string with no escaping. `ndiName` comes from `ndiChannelNames` / `ndiSourcePattern` (operator config, `resolveNdiSourceName`) or from a discovered name. ## src/streaming/streaming-channel-ffmpeg.js
- `RISK` src/streaming/streaming-channel-ffmpeg.js:140 — `buildSrtOutputUrl` silently drops a passphrase that is not 10–79 characters.
